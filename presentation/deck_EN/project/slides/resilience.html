This is the bonus in the brief. Budget, mission count, target time and failures are sliders, and each variant can be stored as a named scenario, copied and compared side by side. For discussion: below 6 million coverage drops fast, above about 7 million more money adds almost nothing. The target time is the strongest lever: going from 4 to 5 minutes saves five sites.
