Air Shield: 27 stations, one network

The optimizer puts every drone where it reaches the most people within 4 minutes, 1 km apart, all controlled from one centre.

Air Shield organises them: 27 docked stations chosen by our optimizer from open data, at least 1 km apart, all run from the Morpheus control centre. Each circle is what one station reaches within four minutes; the honeycomb shows the result per area, darker cells are covered by two stations.
