Tasking, mission decisions and data stay with the requesting agency. Morpheus provides aircraft, remote pilots and the control centre.

No blanket exemption assumed: relief is checked per mission type. Have the legal assessment confirmed by specialist lawyers.

The core point: Morpheus takes on no sovereign tasks, it provides aircraft and pilots. The requesting agency decides on the mission and owns the data. The biggest risk is how the operation is classified: we plan conservatively with the specific category, SORA and an operating authorisation instead of relying on the agency exemption.
